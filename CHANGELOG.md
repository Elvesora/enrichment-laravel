## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Self-contained Laravel 12 and 13 client for the Elvesora Company Enrichment API.
- Immutable enrichment results and credit metadata with idempotency replay information.
- Typed authentication, idempotency, validation, usage-limit, API, response, and transport exceptions.
- Auto-discovered service provider, container binding, namespaced facade, and publishable configuration.
- Local validation for header safety, UTF-8 inputs, finite timeouts, and secret-safe debug output.
- Redirect refusal and retry guidance for transient and malformed gateway responses.
- Mocked HTTP contract coverage, Testbench integration tests, PHPStan, Pint, Composer validation, security auditing, and a supported-version CI matrix.
